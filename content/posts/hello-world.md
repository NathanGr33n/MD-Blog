Welcome to **MD-Blog**, a small markdown-powered blog built with Next.js.

## Why build another blog engine?

Because writing one end to end is a great way to understand the whole content
pipeline:

- Parse frontmatter from Markdown files
- Convert Markdown into safe HTML
- Statically generate a page for every post
